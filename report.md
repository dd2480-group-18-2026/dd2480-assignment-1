# Way of Working (According to Essence)

During the first two lab sessions we decided together on how we should work. This included how we should communicate, which tools we should use and how we should structure the work. We also discussed the expectations on the deliverable based on the assignment instructions. However, there are still potential for improvement in some aspects. For example, we sometimes provide feedback on a PR in discord rather than by leaving a comment on the PR. The checkpoint "The selected practices and tools are used by the whole team" cannot therefore be considered fulfilled. Hence, our team is in the late stage of _In Use_. This said, the two other checkpoints of _In Place_ are fulfilled. As we all regularly raise and discuss potential deviations from our way-of-working.
